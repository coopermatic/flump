Flump — internal Mac build (ad-hoc signed, not notarised)

Use only the ZIP supplied by your build owner after they confirm its validation.
No AIR runtime, Rosetta, Xcode, Terminal or developer account is needed.

1. Quit your existing Flump. Keep that working copy as a backup.
2. Double-click Flump-mac-universal-internal.zip in Finder.
3. Drag the extracted Flump.app to Applications. Keep your backup separately;
   do not replace it until the new app has passed your animation tests.
4. Double-click the new Flump in Applications.
5. If macOS says the developer cannot be verified or Apple cannot check the app:
   close the alert, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and
   click Open Anyway for Flump. Confirm Open and authenticate if asked.
   This is a one-app exception. Later launches should work normally.

If Open Anyway is absent, your Mac may be managed by your organisation. Ask your
build owner for help. If macOS reports damage or malware, stop and report the exact
message. Do not disable security settings or run Terminal commands.

Test a COPY of a known-good Animate project, including its matching published SWF.
Open it in Flump, preview the animation, and export PNG/JSON to a new empty folder.
Ask your build owner to check the output in the existing game before replacing
any working assets. The normal AIR free-tier splash screen may appear.

To check native Apple Silicon execution: open Activity Monitor from Applications
> Utilities, locate Flump, and check that its Kind column says Apple, not Intel.
Report your macOS version and any launch, preview or export problems to the owner.

Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/en-gb/102445
